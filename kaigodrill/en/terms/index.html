Japanese care-work glossary

The terms in Kaigo Drill, grouped by subject. Every kanji has furigana, and each term comes with an easy-Japanese explanation and its meaning in English.

  1. 01Basics of care work13 terms
  2. 02Mind and body14 terms
  3. 03Communication5 terms
  4. 04Daily-living support skills16 terms

01Basics of care work

  • 身体拘束(しんたいこうそく)Physical restraint
  • 手指衛生(しゅしえいせい)Hand hygiene
  • 利用者(りようしゃ)Service user
  • 高齢者虐待(こうれいしゃぎゃくたい)Elder abuse
  • 通報(つうほう)Mandatory report (to the municipality)
  • 尊厳(そんげん)Dignity
  • 標準予防策(ひょうじゅんよぼうさく)Standard precautions
  • 個人防護具(こじんぼうごぐ)Personal protective equipment (PPE)
  • 咳(せき)エチケットCough etiquette
  • 記録(きろく)Written record
  • 被保険者(ひほけんしゃ)Insured person
  • 要介護認定(ようかいごにんてい)Certification of long-term care need
  • 市町村(しちょうそん)The municipality (the insurer)

02Mind and body

  • 誤嚥(ごえん)Aspiration (food or liquid entering the airway)
  • 不顕性誤嚥(ふけんせいごえん)Silent aspiration
  • 嚥下(えんげ)Swallowing
  • 口腔(こうくう)ケアOral care
  • フレイルFrailty
  • 低栄養(ていえいよう)Undernutrition
  • 認知症(にんちしょう)Dementia
  • 中核症状(ちゅうかくしょうじょう)Core symptoms of dementia
  • 見当識障害(けんとうしきしょうがい)Disorientation (to time, place or person)
  • 廃用症候群(はいようしょうこうぐん)Disuse syndrome
  • 閉(と)じこもりBecoming housebound
  • オーラルフレイルOral frailty
  • うつDepression
  • 運動器(うんどうき)The musculoskeletal system

03Communication

  • 報告(ほうこく)Reporting (to a colleague or supervisor)
  • 観察(かんさつ)Observing the user’s condition
  • バイタルサインVital signs
  • 発熱(はつねつ)Fever
  • 傾聴(けいちょう)Listening attentively

04Daily-living support skills

  • 食事(しょくじ)の介助(かいじょ)Meal assistance
  • 姿勢(しせい)Posture
  • 排泄(はいせつ)の介助(かいじょ)Toileting assistance
  • 移乗(いじょう)Transfer (bed ↔ wheelchair)
  • 福祉用具(ふくしようぐ)Assistive equipment
  • 残存機能(ざんぞんきのう)Remaining ability
  • 次亜塩素酸(じあえんそさん)ナトリウムSodium hypochlorite (bleach solution)
  • 嘔吐物(おうとぶつ)Vomit
  • 飛沫(ひまつ)Droplets (from a cough or sneeze)
  • おむつ交換(こうかん)Changing a pad or nappy
  • 入浴(にゅうよく)の介助(かいじょ)Bathing assistance
  • 疥癬(かいせん)Scabies
  • 換気(かんき)Ventilation
  • 腰部保護(ようぶほご)ベルトBack support belt
  • 作業標準(さぎょうひょうじゅん)Written work standard
  • 衛生学的手洗(えいせいがくてきてあら)いHygienic hand washing

Kaigo Drill is a study app developed and run independently by Haven Atelier. It is not affiliated with Prometric Japan Co., Ltd. or Japan’s Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. Questions and explanations are written from public Japanese government sources.
