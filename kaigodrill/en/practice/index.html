Kaigo exam (SSW care): practice questions and exam information

The Specified Skilled Worker (care) evaluation exams are the care skills evaluation test (45 questions, 60 minutes) and the care Japanese-language evaluation test (15 questions, 30 minutes). Looking for past papers? What the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare publishes on its page is sample questions for both tests (linked from “About the exam” below).

This page shows sample questions from Kaigo Drill. Each one comes with its source: materials published by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare and others.

Questions in the form of the care skills evaluation test

The care skills evaluation test is given in the language of the country where it is held. The Japanese original is shown under each question.

  1. Q1When may physical restraint be used as an exception?

    身体拘束(しんたいこうそく)が 例外的(れいがいてき)に 認(みと)められるのは、どんな ときですか。

    1. When the family strongly asks for it
    2. When there are too few staff at night to keep watch
    3. When all three conditions are met
    4. When the user keeps standing up
    Answer and explanation

    Answer: 3. When all three conditions are met

    The three conditions are urgency, no alternative, and temporariness. Only when all are met at once is it allowed as an exception. It is never one person’s decision: the facility confirms it with several staff.

    Source：身体拘束ゼロの実践に向けて 介護施設・事業所における取組手引き（全日本病院協会（厚生労働省 老人保健健康増進等事業））（２）「緊急やむを得ない場合」の３要件

  2. Q2Which statement about aspiration in older people is correct?

    高齢者(こうれいしゃ)の 誤嚥(ごえん)に ついて、正(ただ)しい ものは どれですか。

    1. If there is no choking, no aspiration has happened
    2. Saliva can run into the lungs during sleep
    3. The cough reflex of older people is the same as that of young people
    4. If the mouth is kept clean, aspiration does not happen
    Answer and explanation

    Answer: 2. Saliva can run into the lungs during sleep

    The cough reflex weakens with age, so aspiration can happen with no choking at all — silent aspiration.

    Source：介護現場における感染対策の手引き 第2版（厚生労働省 老健局）８．誤嚥性肺炎（１）特徴

  3. Q3What should a care worker do when a user has a cough, phlegm or a fever?

    利用者(りようしゃ)に 咳(せき)や 痰(たん)、熱(ねつ)が ある とき、介護職員(かいごしょくいん)は どう しますか。

    1. Watch for a few days, then report it
    2. Assume it is just a cold and nurse them yourself
    3. Tell the family when they come
    4. Report it early so a doctor can see them
    Answer and explanation

    Answer: 4. Report it early so a doctor can see them

    When you notice a cough, phlegm or fever, report it early so the person can be seen by a doctor.

    Source：介護現場における感染対策の手引き 第2版（厚生労働省 老健局）８．誤嚥性肺炎（２）日頃の対応（予防法）

  4. Q4Which posture is correct for eating?

    食事(しょくじ)の ときの 姿勢(しせい)で、正(ただ)しい ものは どれですか。

    1. Lift the chin and wash the food down
    2. Sit on the edge of the seat and lean back
    3. Eat with the chin drawn in
    4. Eat with the bed left flat
    Answer and explanation

    Answer: 3. Eat with the chin drawn in

    Keep the chin drawn in; a tipped-back head makes aspiration more likely. Wash the person’s hands before the meal.

    Source：介護現場における感染対策の手引き 第2版（厚生労働省 老健局）図25 各ケアのポイント

Questions in the form of the care Japanese-language evaluation test

The care Japanese-language evaluation test has 5 questions from each of three areas: care vocabulary (choose the word with the same meaning), care conversation and speaking to users (read a conversation and choose what fits) and care documents (read a short document and choose what fits). The sample questions are text only, with furigana on every kanji. As in the app, the translation is shown with the answer.

  1. きょうは 山田(やまだ)さんの「清拭(せいしき)」を します。

    Q5「 」の ことばと 同(おな)じ 意味(いみ)を 選(えら)んで ください。

    1. お風呂(ふろ)で からだを 洗(あら)う
    2. シャンプーで 髪(かみ)を 洗(あら)う
    3. タオルで からだを ふく
    4. 歯(は)ブラシで 歯(は)を みがく
    Answer and explanation

    Answer: 3. タオルで からだを ふく

    Translation

    Today we will do Yamada-san’s 「清拭（せいしき）」.

    Choose the meaning that is the same as the word in 「 」.

    1. Wash the body in the bath

    2. Wash the hair with shampoo

    3. Wipe the body with a towel

    4. Brush the teeth with a toothbrush

    「清拭（せいしき）」 means wiping the body clean with a wet towel or similar. It is done, for example, when the person cannot take a bath. The 拭 in 清拭 is the same kanji as in 拭く (ふく, to wipe). Taking a bath is 「入浴（にゅうよく）」, washing the hair is 「洗髪（せんぱつ）」, and brushing the teeth is 「歯磨き（はみがき）」.

    Source：介護の特定技能評価試験 学習テキスト～介護技能・介護の日本語～ 改訂2版（公益社団法人 日本介護福祉士会（厚生労働省 介護の日本語学習支援等事業））p.230 入浴・清潔保持の介護 ことば（清拭⑧・洗髪⑨・入浴⑬・拭く⑮）・p.231 入浴できない場面で拭く・p.224 歯磨き⑲

  2. 介護職(かいごしょく)：田中(たなか)さん、もうすぐ 昼(ひる)ご飯(はん)です。食堂(しょくどう)へ 行(い)きましょう。

    田中(たなか)さん：きょうは 車(くるま)いすでは なくて、歩(ある)いて 行(い)きたいです。

    介護職(かいごしょく)：いいですね。膝(ひざ)の 痛(いた)みは どうですか。

    田中(たなか)さん：まだ 少(すこ)し 痛(いた)いです。

    介護職(かいごしょく)：では、歩行器(ほこうき)を 使(つか)いましょう。私(わたし)が そばに いますから、ゆっくり 歩(ある)きましょう。

    田中(たなか)さん：はい、そう します。

    Q6会話(かいわ)の 内容(ないよう)で、正(ただ)しい ものを 選(えら)んで ください。

    1. 田中(たなか)さんは、歩行器(ほこうき)を 使(つか)って 食堂(しょくどう)へ 行(い)きます。
    2. 田中(たなか)さんは、車(くるま)いすに 乗(の)って 食堂(しょくどう)へ 行(い)きます。
    3. 田中(たなか)さんは、杖(つえ)を 使(つか)って 食堂(しょくどう)へ 行(い)きます。
    4. 田中(たなか)さんは、膝(ひざ)が 痛(いた)いので 部屋(へや)で 休(やす)みます。
    Answer and explanation

    Answer: 1. 田中(たなか)さんは、歩行器(ほこうき)を 使(つか)って 食堂(しょくどう)へ 行(い)きます。

    Translation

    Care worker: Tanaka-san, it’s almost lunchtime. Let’s go to the dining room.

    Tanaka-san: Today I’d like to walk there, not go in the wheelchair.

    Care worker: That’s good. How is the pain in your knee?

    Tanaka-san: It still hurts a little.

    Care worker: Then let’s use a walker. I’ll stay beside you, so let’s walk slowly.

    Tanaka-san: Yes, I’ll do that.

    Choose the correct statement about the conversation.

    1. Tanaka-san will go to the dining room using a walker.

    2. Tanaka-san will go to the dining room in a wheelchair.

    3. Tanaka-san will go to the dining room using a cane.

    4. Tanaka-san will rest in the room because their knee hurts.

    Tanaka-san’s knee still hurts a little, but they said they want to walk. The care worker said, “Let’s use a walker,” and Tanaka-san answered, “I’ll do that.” 「歩行器（ほこうき）」 is a device that supports the body while walking.

    Source：介護の特定技能評価試験 学習テキスト～介護技能・介護の日本語～ 改訂2版（公益社団法人 日本介護福祉士会（厚生労働省 介護の日本語学習支援等事業））p.208 移動の介護 ことば（車いす⑦・杖⑬・歩行器㉑）・p.205 膝㉙

  3. お知(し)らせ

    ご家族(かぞく)の みなさまへ

    5月(がつ)から、面会(めんかい)の 時間(じかん)を 変更(へんこう)します。

    今(いま)まで：午後(ごご)1時(じ)から 午後(ごご)6時(じ)まで

    5月(がつ)から：午後(ごご)1時(じ)から 午後(ごご)4時(じ)まで

    面会(めんかい)の 前(まえ)に、受付(うけつけ)で お名前(なまえ)を 書(か)いて ください。

    Q7お知(し)らせの 内容(ないよう)で、正(ただ)しい ものを 選(えら)んで ください。

    1. 5月(がつ)から、面会(めんかい)は 午後(ごご)6時(じ)までです。
    2. 5月(がつ)から、面会(めんかい)は 午後(ごご)4時(じ)までです。
    3. 5月(がつ)から、面会(めんかい)の 時間(じかん)が 長(なが)く なります。
    4. 面会(めんかい)の 後(あと)に、受付(うけつけ)で 名前(なまえ)を 書(か)きます。
    Answer and explanation

    Answer: 2. 5月(がつ)から、面会(めんかい)は 午後(ごご)4時(じ)までです。

    Translation

    Notice

    To all families

    From May, the visiting (面会) hours will change (変更).

    Until now: 1 p.m. to 6 p.m.

    From May: 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.

    Before your visit, please write your name at the reception desk.

    Choose the correct statement about the notice.

    1. From May, visits are until 6 p.m.

    2. From May, visits are until 4 p.m.

    3. From May, visiting hours will be longer.

    4. Visitors write their name at reception after the visit.

    From May, visiting hours change to 1 p.m.–4 p.m., two hours shorter than before. Visitors write their name before the visit. 「変更（へんこう）」 means changing something, and 「面会（めんかい）」 means family members or others coming to see a user.

    Source：介護の特定技能評価試験 学習テキスト～介護技能・介護の日本語～ 改訂2版（公益社団法人 日本介護福祉士会（厚生労働省 介護の日本語学習支援等事業））p.245 掲示⑤・p.246 面会・変更・p.258〜259 掲示の書き出し（〜の みなさまへ）

The questions, answers and explanations are taken as they are from the app. In the app, the order of the choices changes every time.

All the questions and the mock exam are in the app

Kaigo Drill has 240 questions in the 4 written subjects of the care skills evaluation test, 27 practice questions for the practical part (judging from pictures), a mock exam in the same format as the real one (45 questions, 60 minutes) and 297 care words.

You can also practise questions in the form of the care Japanese-language evaluation test (3 subjects, 150 questions) and take a Japanese mock exam (15 questions, 30 minutes) in the app.

There is no charge to download it or to use it. It is run on ads.

About the exam

Test names
The care skills evaluation test and the care Japanese-language evaluation test (full names: the Long-Term Care Specified Skilled Worker Evaluation Test (Skills) and (Japanese))Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Accepting Specified Skilled Workers in the Care Field”Test guidelines (skills)Test guidelines (Japanese)
Who sets the test
The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. The tests are delivered and run by an organisation that the ministry subsidises.Test guidelines (skills)
Format
CBT (you answer on a computer at a test centre). As a rule, held every month.Test guidelines (skills)Test guidelines (Japanese)
Where you can take it
In Japan and in other countries. Venues and dates are listed on a dedicated website, reached from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare page.Test guidelines (skills)Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Accepting Specified Skilled Workers in the Care Field”
Test language
The care skills evaluation test is in the language of the country where it is held. The care Japanese-language evaluation test is in Japanese.Test guidelines (skills)Test guidelines (Japanese)
Care skills evaluation test
45 questions, 60 minutes: 40 written questions (basics of care work 10, mind and body 6, communication 4, daily-living support skills 20) and 5 practical questions, in which you look at photos and similar material and judge the correct care steps and the like.Test guidelines (skills)Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Accepting Specified Skilled Workers in the Care Field”
Care Japanese-language evaluation test
15 questions, 30 minutes (care vocabulary 5, care conversation and speaking to users 5, care documents 5)Test guidelines (Japanese)Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Accepting Specified Skilled Workers in the Care Field”
Pass mark
60% or more of the total score (skills), 73% or more (Japanese), from 1 April 2026. The test guidelines say the pass mark is set with adjustments for question difficulty and other factors.Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Accepting Specified Skilled Workers in the Care Field”Test guidelines (skills)
Test fee
About 2,000 yen for each test (for tests from 1 April 2026)Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Notice of revised test fees”
Who can take it
People aged 17 or over (18 or over for Indonesian nationals). To take it in Japan, you need a status of residence.Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Accepting Specified Skilled Workers in the Care Field”
Booking and retaking
You can book up to 60 days ahead. After you take a test, you cannot take the next one for 45 days.Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Accepting Specified Skilled Workers in the Care Field”
Results
Shown on screen at the end of the test. Within about 5 business days, you can get your score report from the dedicated website.Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Accepting Specified Skilled Workers in the Care Field”
Sample questions
Sample questions (PDF) for both tests are on the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare page.Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare: “Accepting Specified Skilled Workers in the Care Field”
