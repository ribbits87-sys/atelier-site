Genba Drill

Study app for the written and practical tests of Japan’s construction Specified Skilled Worker (i) exam

  • iPhoneAvailable
  • AndroidComing soon

The Android version is being prepared for release on Google Play (planned for December 2026). We will announce the release on the Haven Atelier Facebook page.

App version: 1.4.1 (released November 7, 2026)

Learn site safety, laws, types of construction work and site terms, and the tools and machines asked about in the practical test, for Japan’s construction Specified Skilled Worker (i) exam. Questions are in Japanese, as in the real exam, with furigana on every kanji and romaji for each term. Explanations are also available in English, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Tagalog.

06Frequently asked questions

What can I learn with this app?

  • 134 terms and 340 questions (231 multiple-choice, 109 true/false) covering the written test, in 9 chapters
  • 172 picture questions on tools and machines for the practical test, in chapter 10
  • A mock written test (30 questions, 60 minutes) that shows how far you are from the 65% pass line, and your correct-answer rate by chapter

Does it cost anything?

No, there is no charge. You do not pay for the app, there is no monthly fee and there are no in-app purchases. The app is supported by ads.

Which phones does it work on?

It works on iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later). We are getting the Android version ready for release on Google Play (planned for December 2026). We will announce the release on Haven Atelier’s Facebook page.

What languages can I use it in?

Questions are in Japanese, as in the real exam. Kanji have furigana, and terms also have their reading in romaji. Explanations and on-screen text can also be read in Vietnamese, Indonesian, Tagalog and English. You choose your language when you first open the app. After you answer, you can also see the meaning of the question and the choices in Vietnamese or English.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

Yes. Terms, questions and explanations are stored in the app, so flashcards, quizzes, mock exams and reviews all work without a connection.

What are the questions based on?

The terms and questions for the written test are based on Japanese law (e-Gov) and published materials of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism and others. The picture questions on tools for the practical test cover the tools and machines in JAC’s published practical textbooks that are common to all three categories. Every term and question shows its source.

Is the mock exam in the same format as the real exam?

The mock written test has 30 questions in 60 minutes, as in the real exam, and the same 65% pass line. The questions are a mix of true/false and 4-option multiple choice.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your study records are saved on your phone. You can send your progress card (mock exam scores, correct-answer rate by chapter and days studied) as an image or as text to anyone you choose.
