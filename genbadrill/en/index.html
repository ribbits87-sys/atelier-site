Genba Drill

Study app for the written test of Japan’s construction Specified Skilled Worker (i) exam

  • iPhoneAvailable
  • AndroidComing soon

The Android version is being prepared for release on Google Play (planned for December 2026). We will announce the release on the Haven Atelier Facebook page.

Learn site safety, laws, types of construction work and site terms for Japan’s construction Specified Skilled Worker (i) written test. Questions are in Japanese, as in the real exam, with furigana on every kanji and romaji for each term. Explanations are also available in English, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Tagalog.

App Store (iPhone)Android: coming soon

01On this site

  • Glossary

    Site terms one by one: furigana, easy Japanese and the meaning in English.

02About the app

  • 134 terms and 340 questions (231 multiple-choice, 109 true/false) across 9 chapters
  • Mock exam: 30 questions in 60 minutes, with your score against the 65% pass line and by chapter
  • Questions you get wrong come back just before you would forget them (spaced repetition)
  • Every term and question shows its source (the article of law or ministry material)
  • Works offline. Free, with ads

03Chapters

  1. 01Health & safety basics11 terms
  2. 02PPE & fall prevention9 terms
  3. 03Scaffolding & work at height18 terms
  4. 04Machinery & electrical safety9 terms
  5. 05Housekeeping (5S)7 terms
  6. 06Laws & regulations21 terms
  7. 07Greetings & manners11 terms
  8. 08Types of construction work21 terms
  9. 09Construction methods & site terms27 terms

04How we make it

Terms, questions and explanations are written by Haven Atelier in our own words, based on Japanese law (e-Gov) and published materials of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare and the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism. We do not copy text from the official exam textbooks or sample questions.

If you find a mistake, please email us. Corrections will be listed publicly in a correction log.
