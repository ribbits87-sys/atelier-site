Chapter 8　Types of construction work

土木一式工事(どぼくいっしきこうじ)

dobokuisshikikōji

MeaningGeneral civil engineering work

The app has 3 questions on this term.

App Store (iPhone)

In easy Japanese

海(うみ)・川(かわ)・山林(さんりん)など、自然(しぜん)を 相手(あいて)にした 工事(こうじ)です。ダム・道路(どうろ)・橋(はし)・上下水道(じょうげすいどう)などが あります。

Work dealing with the natural world — sea, rivers, mountains — such as dams, roads, bridges, and water/sewage systems.

Source

  • 建設業法 ／ 国土交通省（e-Gov 法令検索） 別表第一

Source checked: 2026-09-05　Sources are listed under their official Japanese names.

Practise with the app

Genba Drill has 3 questions on this term. Questions are in Japanese, as in the real exam, and the explanations can be read in English.
