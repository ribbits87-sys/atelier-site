Chapter 5　Housekeeping (5S)

清掃(せいそう)

seisō

MeaningSeiso — cleaning

Practise with 340 questions in the app.

App Store (iPhone)

In easy Japanese

掃除(そうじ)を して、現場(げんば)を きれいに 保(たも)つ ことです。

Cleaning, and keeping the site clean.

Source

  • 職場のあんぜんサイト（安全衛生キーワード） ／ 厚生労働省

Practise with the app

Genba Drill lets you review term cards and questions again and again, with furigana. Explanations can be read in easy Japanese and English.

  • Works offline
