Chapter 7　Greetings & manners

ご安全(あんぜん)に

goanzenni

MeaningGreeting wishing everyone safety

The app has 3 questions on this term.

App Store (iPhone)

In easy Japanese

現場(げんば)で 使(つか)う あいさつです。おたがいの 安全(あんぜん)を 願(ねが)う 気持(きも)ちを 伝(つた)えます。

A greeting used on site, expressing a wish for each other’s safety.

Source

  • 職場のあんぜんサイト（安全衛生キーワード） ／ 厚生労働省

Source checked: 2026-09-03　Sources are listed under their official Japanese names.

Practise with the app

Genba Drill has 3 questions on this term. Questions are in Japanese, as in the real exam, and the explanations can be read in English.
