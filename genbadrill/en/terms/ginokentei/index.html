Chapter 6　Laws & regulations

技能検定(ぎのうけんてい)

ginōkentei

MeaningTrade skill test

The app has 3 questions on this term.

App Store (iPhone)

In easy Japanese

働(はたら)く 人(ひと)の 技能(ぎのう)の 程度(ていど)を 検定(けんてい)する 制度(せいど)です。実技試験(じつぎしけん)と 学科試験(がっかしけん)に よって 行(おこな)われます。

A system that certifies a worker’s level of skill, carried out through a practical test and a written test.

Source

  • 職業能力開発促進法 ／ 厚生労働省（e-Gov 法令検索） 第44条

Source checked: 2026-09-05　Sources are listed under their official Japanese names.

Practise with the app

Genba Drill has 3 questions on this term. Questions are in Japanese, as in the real exam, and the explanations can be read in English.

Only in the app

  • 340 questions (231 multiple-choice, 109 true/false) in Japanese, as in the real exam
  • A mock exam: 30 questions in 60 minutes, as in the real test
  • Questions you get wrong come back just before you would forget them
  • Works offline
