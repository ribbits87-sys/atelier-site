Japanese construction site glossary

The terms in Genba Drill, grouped by chapter. Every kanji has furigana, and each term comes with an easy-Japanese explanation and its meaning in English.

Learned the words? Test yourself

In the Genba Drill app, you answer exam-style questions that use these terms.

Only in the app

  • 340 questions (231 multiple-choice, 109 true/false) in Japanese, as in the real exam
  • A mock exam: 30 questions in 60 minutes, as in the real test
  • Questions you get wrong come back just before you would forget them
  • Works offline

01Health & safety basics

  • 危険予知活動(きけんよちかつどう)KY activity (hazard prediction)
  • ヒヤリ・ハットNear miss
  • 立入禁止(たちいりきんし)No entry / keep out
  • 作業主任者(さぎょうしゅにんしゃ)Operations chief / work supervisor
  • 特別教育(とくべつきょういく)Special education (short safety course)
  • 技能講習(ぎのうこうしゅう)Skills training course (licensed)
  • 新規入場者教育(しんきにゅうじょうしゃきょういく)New-entrant site induction
  • 指差呼称(ゆびさしこしょう)Point-and-call
  • 悪天候(あくてんこう)Bad weather (defined thresholds)
  • 安全衛生教育(あんぜんえいせいきょういく)Safety and health education
  • 熱中症(ねっちゅうしょう)Heat illness / heat stroke
