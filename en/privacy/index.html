01Information on this website

This website has no accounts and no contact forms. It uses no analytics (such as Google Analytics) and no ads.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. As with any website, GitHub’s servers may keep access records (IP address, time, browser type and so on) when a page is shown. We do not receive these records and do not use them to identify anyone.

Text is displayed with Google Fonts. When a page opens, your browser loads font data from Google’s servers, which sends your IP address and similar information to Google. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Your furigana on/off setting is stored only in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

02Information we receive when you contact us

When you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address, your name if you give it, and the content of your message.

We use it only to reply and to deal with what you wrote (for example, checking and fixing a problem in an app or a mistake in a question).

Enquiry e-mails reach us through a mail-forwarding service (ImprovMX) and are received and kept in Google’s Gmail, in an account used only by the operator.

03How we keep it safe

Only the operator reads and handles enquiry e-mails.

The e-mail account is protected by two-step verification so that no one but the operator can use it.

Mail forwarding is provided by ImprovMX Incorporated and Gmail by Google LLC, both in the United States. We take these measures with the United States’ data-protection rules in mind.

04Sharing with third parties

We do not give your personal information to third parties without your consent, except where the law requires it.

05Access, correction and deletion

To ask us to disclose, correct, stop using or delete information about you, please contact us at the address below. We will confirm that the request comes from you and respond without delay.

06Other websites

Once you leave this site for another one, such as the App Store, Facebook, e-Gov or a government ministry, that site’s own policy applies.
