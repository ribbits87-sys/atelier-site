Kirikae Drill

Practice for the foreign license conversion (gaimen kirikae) knowledge test

  • iPhoneAvailable
  • AndroidPlanned

App version: 1.0.2 (released November 13, 2026)

Kirikae Drill helps you prepare for the knowledge test you take when converting a foreign driver’s license to a Japanese one (gaimen kirikae). Questions are in Japanese with furigana you can switch on and off, with English, Vietnamese and Chinese translations underneath.

App Store (iPhone)Android: planned

Features

  • 505 true-or-false questions for the knowledge test, each showing the part of the Traffic Rules Guide it is based on
  • Japanese with switchable furigana, and English, Vietnamese and Chinese translations underneath
  • A 50-question mock exam, the same length as the real test (45 or more correct answers to pass)
  • Review that brings back the questions you missed just as you are about to forget them, timed to your test date
  • A picture guide to road signs and road markings (questions on markings come with figures)
  • Covers the amendments that took effect in September 2026, such as the 30 km/h legal speed limit on residential roads

About the exam

Foreign license conversion (gaimen kirikae)
The procedure by which someone who holds a driver’s license from another country or region gets a Japanese driver’s license with part of the written and driving tests waived (Road Traffic Act, Article 97-2, paragraph 3)National Police Agency: “For holders of a foreign driver’s license” (外国の運転免許をお持ちの方)
Knowledge test
A check of the knowledge needed to drive. It is taken instead of the written testNational Police Agency: “Revision of the foreign license conversion procedure”
Where to take it
At a driver’s license center or test site of the prefectural police for your address in Japan. You apply in personNational Police Agency: “For holders of a foreign driver’s license” (外国の運転免許をお持ちの方)
Number of questions
50National Police Agency: “Revision of the foreign license conversion procedure”
Pass mark
90% or more (45 or more out of 50). The same standard as for people getting a license for the first timeNational Police Agency: “Revision of the foreign license conversion procedure”
Time limit
30 minutesOkayama Prefecture: information from the Driver’s License Division, Traffic Department (交通部運転免許課)
Languages
Japanese and 20 foreign languages (English, Spanish, Persian, Korean, Chinese, Portuguese, Russian, Thai, Tagalog, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Khmer, Nepali, Burmese, Mongolian, Ukrainian, Sinhala, Urdu, Arabic and Hindi)Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department: “Written tests you can take in a foreign language”
Test days for each language
Varies by prefecture. For example, the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department offers the knowledge test in all 20 foreign languages every weekday at the Fuchu and Samezu test centresTokyo Metropolitan Police Department: “Written tests you can take in a foreign language”
Exempt countries and regions
For some countries and regions, the knowledge test and the driving skills test are waived (29 countries and regions, according to the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department)Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department: “How to convert a driver’s license obtained abroad to a Japanese driver’s license”
Driving skills test
Since October 2025, tasks such as going through a crosswalk have been added, and scoring is now the same as for people getting a license for the first timeNational Police Agency: “Revision of the foreign license conversion procedure”

Frequently asked questions

What can I do with the app?

You can solve 505 questions chapter by chapter. You can review just the questions you got wrong, the ones you find difficult or the ones you marked. There is also a signs and road markings guide covering 47 types of signs and 14 types of road markings. The home screen shows how many days are left until your test.

How much does it cost?

Right after you download it, you can use some of the questions in each chapter and one mock exam. “Unlock all questions” is a one-time purchase of 1,200 yen, with no monthly fee. After you buy it, you keep using your study records from before.

Which devices can I use it on?

You can use it on iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later). We plan to release an Android version.

Which languages can I use it in?

Questions are in Japanese (with furigana), with a translation in English, Vietnamese or Chinese (Simplified) shown underneath. The words on the screen are also shown in Japanese and the language you choose. You can switch languages at any time.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. The questions, explanations and the signs and road markings guide are inside the app, so you can solve questions even where there is no signal.

What are the questions based on?

They are based on the National Public Safety Commission’s Traffic Rules Guide (交通の方法に関する教則), published by the National Police Agency, and on the Road Traffic Act, its Enforcement Order and its Enforcement Regulations. The Traffic Rules Guide is reflected up to the amendments that took effect on 1 September 2026 (such as the 30 km/h legal speed limit on residential roads). Each explanation shows the item of the Traffic Rules Guide or the law it is based on.

What is the mock exam like?

Like the real test, it has 50 questions and a 30-minute time limit, and 45 or more correct is the guide for passing (you can change the time in Settings). Once you buy, you can take it as many times as you like, with 50 questions drawn from all the questions each time. On the results screen, you can read the explanations for every question and see which chapters you are weak in.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your study records are saved on your device. When you change phones, use “Restore purchases” (購入を復元) to get back what you bought.
