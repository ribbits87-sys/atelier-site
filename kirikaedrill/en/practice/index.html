Gaimen kirikae knowledge test: practice questions and test information

When you convert a foreign driver’s license to a Japanese one (gaimen kirikae), you take a knowledge test instead of the written test. From October 1, 2025, the knowledge test has 50 questions, and you pass with 45 or more correct answers (90% or more).

This page shows eight questions from Kirikae Drill. The app’s questions are true-or-false questions based on the National Public Safety Commission’s Traffic Rules Guide (交通の方法に関する教則) and laws such as the Road Traffic Act, and each one comes with its sources. The Japanese original is shown under each question.

Sample questions (true or false)

  1. Q1On an ordinary road with no speed limit signs or markings, motor vehicles may go up to 60 km/h even on a narrow road without a center line.

    最高速度の標識や標示がない一般道路であれば、中央線がない狭い道路でも、自動車は時速60キロメートルまで出してよい。

    Answer true or false.

    Answer and explanation

    Answer: False

    From September 1, 2026, even on ordinary roads with no posted speed limit, the legal speed limit for motor vehicles is 30 km/h on roads that have neither a center line nor traffic lanes and are not divided into the two directions. 60 km/h applies to ordinary roads with a center line and the like.

    Key pointOrdinary roads with no center line and no lanes: 30 km/h. It is not 60 everywhere.

    Source：国家公安委員会「交通の方法に関する教則」第5章第4節1(1)／教則の改正（令和8年国家公安委員会告示第32号、2026年9月1日施行）／道路交通法 第22条／道路交通法施行令 第11条

  2. Q2When turning right at an intersection, you should move in advance as close as possible to the center of the road, go around the outside of the center of the intersection in a wide arc, and turn slowly.

    交差点を右に曲がるときは、前もって道路の中央にできるだけ寄り、交差点の中心の外側を大きく回って、徐行しながら曲がる。

    Answer true or false.

    Answer and explanation

    Answer: False

    For a right turn, move to the center of the road in advance and turn slowly, passing "just inside" the center of the intersection. You do not go around the outside of the center in a wide arc (mopeds making a two-step turn are different).

    Key pointRight turn: move to the center, go slowly "just inside" the center of the intersection.

    Source：国家公安委員会「交通の方法に関する教則」第5章第7節2(2)／道路交通法 第34条

  3. Q3When turning left at an intersection, you should move in advance as close as possible to the left edge of the road, and turn along the corner of the intersection while going slowly (at a speed that lets you stop immediately).

    交差点を左に曲がるときは、前もって道路の左端にできるだけ寄り、交差点の角に沿って、徐行（すぐ止まれる速度で進むこと）をしながら曲がる。

    Answer true or false.

    Answer and explanation

    Answer: True

    For a left turn, move to the left edge of the road in advance, and turn slowly so as to follow the left corner of the intersection.

    Key pointLeft turn: keep to the left edge and go slowly along the corner.

    Source：国家公安委員会「交通の方法に関する教則」第5章第7節2(1)／道路交通法 第34条

  4. Q4When about to turn right at an intersection, you should take great care because two-wheeled vehicles coming straight in the oncoming lane can be hard to see.

    交差点で右折しようとするときは、対向車線を直進してくる二輪車が見えにくくなることがあるので、十分注意する。

    Answer true or false.

    Answer and explanation

    Answer: True

    When turning right, motorcycles and other two-wheelers coming straight in the oncoming lane may be hidden behind other vehicles and hard to see. Check carefully before turning.

    Key pointTurning right: watch for oncoming two-wheelers going straight.

    Source：国家公安委員会「交通の方法に関する教則」第5章第7節1(1)／道路交通法 第36条

  5. Q5When crossing a railroad crossing without traffic lights, you should come to a stop before the crossing (before the stop line if there is one) and check with your own eyes and ears that no train is coming from the left or right.

    信号機のない踏切を渡るときは、踏切の手前（停止線があればその手前）で一度止まり、自分の目と耳で左右から列車が来ないかを確かめる。

    Answer true or false.

    Answer and explanation

    Answer: True

    Serious accidents tend to happen at railroad crossings, so always stop before them. Open the window, for example, and not only look but also listen to check that no train is coming from the left or right.

    Key pointRailroad crossing: stop just before it, check with eyes and ears.

    Source：国家公安委員会「交通の方法に関する教則」第6章第1節1(1)／道路交通法 第33条

  6. Q6A person walking while pushing a standard-size motorcycle follows the same traffic rules as a pedestrian.

    普通自動二輪車を押して歩いている人は、歩行者と同じ交通規則に従う。

    Answer true or false.

    Answer and explanation

    Answer: True

    A person who has got off and is walking while pushing a two-wheeled motor vehicle, a two-wheeled moped, a specified small motorized bicycle or a bicycle is treated as a pedestrian.

    Key pointSomeone walking and pushing a two-wheeler or bicycle is a "pedestrian".

    Source：国家公安委員会「交通の方法に関する教則」第2章第1節7／道路交通法 第2条

  7. Q7Even if your driver's license has the condition "glasses, etc. required", you do not have to wear glasses if you are only driving around your neighborhood for a short time.

    運転免許証に「眼鏡等使用」の条件が記載されている人でも、近所を短時間運転するだけなら眼鏡をかけなくてもよい。

    Answer true or false.

    Answer and explanation

    Answer: False

    The Traffic Rules Guide (as amended by Notice No. 47 of 2024) asks you to check before driving that you are following the conditions written on your driver's license or recorded on your My Number license card (such as "glasses, etc. required"). You must follow them regardless of distance or time.

    Key pointAlways follow your license conditions (glasses etc.).

    Source：国家公安委員会「交通の方法に関する教則」第4章第1節1(1)ウ／道路交通法 第91条

  8. Q8If the car has an airbag, the passenger in the front seat does not need to wear a seat belt.

    エアバッグが付いている自動車であれば、助手席の同乗者にシートベルトを着用させる必要はない。

    Answer true or false.

    Answer and explanation

    Answer: False

    The Traffic Rules Guide says that the driver must wear a seat belt and must make passengers in the front and back seats wear them too, and that this applies even when the car has airbags.

    Key pointSeat belts are needed even with airbags.

    Source：国家公安委員会「交通の方法に関する教則」第5章第1節3(1)／道路交通法 第71条の3

The questions, answers and explanations are taken as they are from the app (version 1.0.5). In the app, each question is in Japanese with furigana, with the English, Vietnamese or Chinese translation underneath.

All 859 questions and the mock exam are in the app

Kirikae Drill has 859 questions by chapter, a 50-question, 30-minute mock exam (45 or more correct answers to pass), review of the questions you missed, and a picture guide to road signs and road markings.

About the exam

Foreign license conversion (gaimen kirikae)
The procedure by which someone who holds a driver’s license from another country or region gets a Japanese driver’s license with part of the written and driving tests waived (Road Traffic Act, Article 97-2, paragraph 3)National Police Agency: “For holders of a foreign driver’s license” (外国の運転免許をお持ちの方)
Knowledge test
A check of the knowledge needed to drive. It is taken instead of the written testNational Police Agency: “Revision of the foreign license conversion procedure”
Where to take it
At a driver’s license center or test site of the prefectural police for your address in Japan. You apply in personNational Police Agency: “For holders of a foreign driver’s license” (外国の運転免許をお持ちの方)
Number of questions
50National Police Agency: “Revision of the foreign license conversion procedure”National Police Agency notice: revision of the “Guidelines for handling foreign license matters” (外国免許等関係事務取扱い要領), January 8, 2026
Question format
Text-based questions (文章形式)National Police Agency notice: revision of the “Guidelines for handling foreign license matters” (外国免許等関係事務取扱い要領), January 8, 2026
Pass mark
90% or more (45 or more out of 50). The same standard as for people getting a license for the first timeNational Police Agency: “Revision of the foreign license conversion procedure”National Police Agency notice: revision of the “Guidelines for handling foreign license matters” (外国免許等関係事務取扱い要領), January 8, 2026
Time limit
Up to 30 minutesNational Police Agency notice: revision of the “Guidelines for handling foreign license matters” (外国免許等関係事務取扱い要領), January 8, 2026
Languages
Japanese and 20 foreign languages (English, Spanish, Persian, Korean, Chinese, Portuguese, Russian, Thai, Tagalog, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Khmer, Nepali, Burmese, Mongolian, Ukrainian, Sinhala, Urdu, Arabic and Hindi)Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department: “Written tests you can take in a foreign language”
Test days for each language
Varies by prefecture. For example, the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department offers the knowledge test in all 20 foreign languages every weekday at the Fuchu and Samezu test centresTokyo Metropolitan Police Department: “Written tests you can take in a foreign language”
Exempt countries and regions
For some countries and regions, the knowledge test and the driving skills test are waived (29 countries and regions, according to the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department)Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department: “How to convert a driver’s license obtained abroad to a Japanese driver’s license”
Driving skills test
Since October 2025, tasks such as going through a crosswalk have been added, and scoring is now the same as for people getting a license for the first timeNational Police Agency: “Revision of the foreign license conversion procedure”
How to apply
How to book (online, by phone, etc.) varies by prefecture.
